title: Use of AI
---
This page lists the AI tools our team used during the project and what we used them for. AI served as a support tool for **writing, research, brainstorming, coding, design, meeting notes and outreach**.&#x20;

All AI output was reviewed by team members, and the decisions and conclusions in this project are our own.

## BioRender

* **Scientific illustrations:** using BioRender's AI features to help create figures. The AI helped with layout, flowchart design and icon suggestions. The team decided the content and checked that every label, structure and step is scientifically correct.&#x20;

## ChatGPT (OpenAI)

* **Coloring book:** development of *The Tiny Helpers Inside You*. It helped brainstorm ideas for the story and activities, and visualize how different scenes could be illustrated so that the intended message is easy for young children to understand. The suggestions and visualizations served as inspiration. The team used these ideas to guide the design and refine the booklet for the target audience of children aged 3 to 5.
* **Writing:** making text shorter, more concrete and more readable, and improving grammar.

## Claude (Anthropic)

* **Background research:** searching literature.
* **Design (Claude Design):** creating and editing the flyers for PMOS Awareness Month in Claude Design.
* **Modeling:** used to structure the working directories, troubleshoot errors, and paraphrase the content for the wiki.
* **PMOS Awareness workshop:** brainstorming ideas and building a flow for the chosen idea.
* **PMOS Surveys:** helping to find clinicians who treat PMOS to send the survey to, structuring and revising the survey questions, and making the wiki parts shorter and more concrete.
* **Public outreach:** writing emails for Human Practices and Education.
* **References:** formatting references in APA 7 and scanning texts for consistent reference style.
* **Safety:** understanding the different kinds of safety for a therapeutic project and identifying the regulations that apply to our project and for ideas to design flowchart.
* **Sponsorship:** finding wet lab sponsors and sponsors for souvenirs (hoodies and stickers) in and around the Nordic region.
* **Sustainability:** formatting, sentence flow improvement.
* **Wiki coding:** helping with repository setup and the <Tooltip text="An automated process that builds, tests and publishes the website every time the code changes.">CI/CD pipeline</Tooltip>, generating code for many components (especially complex ones) and advanced CSS, analyzing the wiki’s composition and suggesting missing features, debugging deployment errors, and implementing caching.
* **Writing:** grammar correction and improving sentence flow. Generating alt text for figures.

## Hermes (Cultivarium)

* **Assistance with molecular design:** help with <Tooltip text="Ribosome binding site: the stretch of mRNA where the ribosome attaches to start making protein.">RBS</Tooltip> to <Tooltip text="The three-letter code (usually AUG) where protein production begins.">start codon</Tooltip> distance length determination (recommendation of online tools for RBS (RBS Calculator v2.0)).

* **Wet lab:** help with troubleshooting, general help (e.g., how many primers to order)
