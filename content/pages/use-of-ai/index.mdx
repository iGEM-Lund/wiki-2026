## Introduction

This page lists the AI tools our team used during the project and what we used them for. AI served as a support tool for writing, brainstorming, research and coding. All AI output was reviewed by team members, and the decisions and conclusions in this project are our own.

## Hermes (Cultivarium)

* **Writing:** grammar correction and improving sentence flow, structuring of the sentences/paragraph. Generating ALT text for figures.
* **Background research:** searching recent literature.

## Claude (Anthropic)

* **Writing:** grammar correction and improving sentence flow. Generating ALT text for figures.
* **Background research:** searching literature.
* **Wiki coding:** helping with repository setup and the CI/CD pipeline, generating code for many components (especially complex ones) and advanced CSS, analysing the wiki's composition and suggesting missing features, debugging deployment errors, and implementing caching.
* **PMOS Awareness workshop:** brainstorming ideas and building a flow for the chosen idea.
* **Sponsorship:** finding wet lab sponsors and sponsors for souvenirs (hoodies and stickers) in and around the Nordic region.
* **Safety:** understanding the different kinds of safety for a therapeutic project and identifying the regulations that apply to our project.
* **Modeling:** used for structuring the working directories and troubleshooting errors.
* **PMOS Surveys**: helping to find clinicians who treat PMOS to send the survey to, structuring and revising the survey texts and questions, and making whole parts shorter / more concrete. Regarding data analysis, we exported the response data from the survey tool and gave Claude the Excel file. Claude counted the answers per question and built the figures from those counts as editable Excel charts. It also drafted figure captions, wrote the alt texts, and shortened our wiki survey texts.
* **References**: formatting references in APA 7 and scanning texts for consistent reference style.
* **Design (Claude Design):** creating and editing the flyers for PMOS Awareness Month in Claude Design.

## Gemini 3.6 Flash (Google)

* **Public outreach:** writing emails for Human Practices and Education.
* **Coloring book:** development of *The Tiny Helpers Inside You*. It helped brainstorm ideas for the story and activities, and visualise how different scenes could be illustrated so that the intended message is easy for young children to understand. The suggestions and visualisations served as inspiration. The team used these ideas to guide the design and refine the booklet for the target audience of children aged 3 to 5.
