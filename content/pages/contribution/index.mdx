AnTigone is a hypothesis-stage project, so what we can **contribute** is mainly the material we built or documented along the way.&#x20;

Everything below is **free to download and reuse** under <Tooltip text="Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International; an internationally recognized copyright license">CC BY 4.0</Tooltip>.

### Biological Parts

During the project, we designed <Tooltip text="Basic parts are single functional DNA elements, such as a promoter, a tag or a coding sequence">**six new basic parts:**</Tooltip>

* [BBa\_261I5W2C](https://registry.igem.org/parts/bba-261i5w2c) - 5β-reductase ci2350 from *Clostridium innocuum*.
* [BBa\_262E3BYN](https://registry.igem.org/parts/bba-262e3byn) - double mutant of 5β-reductase ci2350 (R215A + V263I).
* [BBa\_26SIUTOE](https://registry.igem.org/parts/bba-26siutoe) - 5β-reductase ci2350 with poly-adenine regions removed via <Tooltip text="DNA changes that do not change the amino acid sequence of the protein.">silent mutations</Tooltip> to prevent protein truncation.
* [BBa\_26ZLKFI1](https://registry.igem.org/parts/bba-26zlkfi1) - Human steroid-5β-reductase, encoded by the human gene AKR1D1.
* [BBa\_26ZQPIAC](https://registry.igem.org/parts/bba-26zqpiac) - Maltose-Binding Protein (MBP) engineered <Tooltip text="A protein partner fused to the target to help it stay dissolved and fold properly.">solubility tag.</Tooltip>
* [BBa\_26P9V9ID](https://registry.igem.org/parts/bba-26p9v9id) - <Tooltip text="A DNA sequence that tells the cell's machinery to stop copying the gene.">Transcription terminator</Tooltip> t0 from <Tooltip text="A virus that infects bacteria.">bacteriophage</Tooltip> lambda, with a 35 bp spacer placed after the <Tooltip text="A three-letter code in a gene that tells the cell to end protein production.">stop codon</Tooltip>, for efficient transcription termination.

Using these new basic parts, as well as older basic parts designed by other iGEM teams, a total of <Tooltip text="Composite parts are made by joining several basic parts together.">**12 new composite parts**</Tooltip> were created that helped us with our goal of reducing testosterone into its less active form, <Tooltip text="The form of testosterone after 5β-reduction. It is less active as an androgen.">5β-dihydrotestosterone</Tooltip>, using <Tooltip text="Enzymes that add hydrogen to a steroid at the 5β position, changing the molecule's 3D shape.">steroid-5β-reductases</Tooltip>.

All parts and the construct architectures are on our [Parts](/parts) page.
