## Parts

This page summarizes our contributions to the Registry of Standard Biological Parts. A full description of each part is available in the iGEM Parts Registry.

During the project, we designed new basic and composite parts, which can be divided into two categories: &#x20;

* 4 new basic parts and 9 composite parts for the expression of testosterone reducing enzymes;

- &#x20;2 composite parts for the hypoxia gate system.

### **Enzymes**

ci2350

AKR1D1

mutant ci2350

### **Solubility Tags**

Trx

SUMO

MBP

### **Gate**
