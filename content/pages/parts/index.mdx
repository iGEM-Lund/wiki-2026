## Parts

This page summarizes our contributions to the Registry of Standard Biological Parts. Full description of each part is available in the [iGEM Parts Registry](https://registry.igem.org/), reachable by clicking <Tooltip text="The prefix of part IDs in the iGEM Registry.">BBa</Tooltip> codes below.

During the project, we designed <Tooltip text="Basic parts are single functional DNA elements, such as a gene, a promoter or a tag.">**4 new basic**</Tooltip> and <Tooltip text="Composite parts are made by joining several basic parts together.">**12 new composite parts**</Tooltip>, that help us with our goal of reducing testosterone into its less active form, <Tooltip text="The form of testosterone after 5β-reduction. It is less active as an androgen.">5β-dihydrotestosterone</Tooltip>, using <Tooltip text="Enzymes that add hydrogen to a steroid at the 5β position, changing the molecule's 3D shape.">steroid-5β-reductases</Tooltip>.

### **Enzymes**

**ci2350**

<Tooltip text="Needing NADH, a molecule that carries electrons, as a helper.">NADH-dependent</Tooltip> 5β-reductase, first identified in *Clostridium innocuum*, that catalyzes the reduction of progesterone to 5β-dihydroprogesterone \[[citation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61425-6)]. It has broad activity on steroid hormones (<Tooltip text="Steroids with a double bond at position 4 and a ketone (C=O) at position 3, such as testosterone and progesterone.">Δ⁴-3-ketosteroids</Tooltip>), due to their structural similarity,  among which is testosterone, which is also readily reduced to 5β-dihydrotestosterone, resulting in lesser androgenic effect.

<MoleculeViewer modelUrl="https://static.igem.wiki/teams/6160/wiki/dry-lab-3d-model/c12350.glb" format="glb" style="cartoon" caption="AlphaFold v2.2.0 model of ci2350 from *Clostridium innocuum*. Per-residue confidence exceeded 90 (very high) across the model." refId="mod-iwdp3" />

**ci2350\_R215A:V263I**

A double mutant of ci2350 that was modeled to shift the specificity from progesterone towards testosterone (see [Modelling](/modeling) for more details).

<MoleculeViewer format="pdb" style="cartoon" highlightLabel="Alanine (Ala): the mutated residue, replacing arginine" caption="Predicted 3D structure of ci2350_R215A:V263I (AlphaFold)." refId="mod-90g4q" />

**AKR1D1**

<Tooltip text="Needing NADPH, a molecule that carries electrons, as a helper.">NADPH-dependent</Tooltip> steroid-5β-reductase of human origin, primarily expressed in the liver, that catalyzes the reduction of Δ⁴-3-ketosteroids. Similar to ci2350, it has broad activity on steroid hormones.

<MoleculeViewer modelUrl="https://static.igem.wiki/teams/6160/wiki/parts/3d-akr1d1.glb" format="glb" style="cartoon" caption="3D structure of AKR1D1 in complex with NADP+ (PDB ID: 3DOP)." refId="mod-kpkf6" />
