## Parts

This page summarizes our contributions to the Registry of Standard Biological Parts. Full description of each part is available in the [iGEM Parts Registry](https://registry.igem.org/), reachable by clicking BBa codes below.

During the project, we designed **4 new basic** and **9 new composite parts**, that help us with our goal of reducing testosterone  into its less active form, 5β-dihydrotestosterone, using steroid-5β-reductases.

### **Enzymes**

**ci2350**

NADH-dependent 5β-reductase, first identified in *Clostridium innocuum*, that catalyzes the reduction of progesterone to 5β-dihydroprogesterone \[[citation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61425-6)]. It has broad activity on steroid hormones (Δ⁴-3-ketosteroids), due to their structural similarity,  among which is testosterone, that is also readily reduced to 5β-dihydrotestosterone, resulting in lesser androgenic effect.

<MoleculeViewer modelUrl="https://static.igem.wiki/teams/6160/wiki/parts/3d-ci2350.glb" format="glb" style="cartoon" caption="Predicted 3D structure of ci2350 (AlphaFold)." refId="mod-86w38" />

**ci2350\_R215A:V263I**

A double mutant of ci2350 that was modeled to shift the specificity from progesterone towards testosterone (see [Modelling](/technical_modeling) for more details).

<MoleculeViewer format="pdb" style="cartoon" highlightLabel="Alanine (Ala): the mutated residue, replacing arginine" caption="Predicted 3D structure of ci2350_R215A:V263I (AlphaFold)." refId="mod-90g4q" />

&#x20;&#x20;

**AKR1D1**

NADPH-dependent steroid-5β-reductase of human origin, primarily expressed in the liver, that catalyzes the reduction of Δ⁴-3-ketosteroids. Similar to ci2350, it has broad activity on steroid hormones.
