## Safety in the lab

All the members who will be performing experiments in the laboratory are given the required safety training and completing the safety training was made mandatory before they are allowed to work in teh laboratory.

&#x20;

## Waste Management

All the biological and chemical wastes resulting from our experiments are properly labelled, segregated and disposed off by following Lund University's waste management guidelines (LINK):

* **Biological wastes** are autoclaved at 100°C for 25 minutes before they are disposed off along with the general wastes to ensure that no microbial contamination is possible.
* **Chemical wastes** are segregated in dedicated chemical waste containers found inside the chemical waste cabinets and are disposed off by the university using **certified hazardous waste contractors**.
* **Glass wastes**, that are not contaminated with any harmful chemicals or biological sources are collected in dedicated bins before they are disposed along with regular glass wastes.
