<Quote quote="Sustainable development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs." quotee="UN's Brundtland Commission, report &#x22;Our Common Future&#x22;, 1987" />

Most people associate sustainability primarily with environmental protection, reducing emissions, conserving resources, and protecting ecosystems. However, this represents only one of three essential pillars. Economic viability and social equity are equally important. A truly sustainable solution must be economically feasible to implement and scale, while also promoting fair access to resources and opportunities. All three pillars must work together, addressing one while ignoring the others often creates new problems. Thus, this sustainable development page should be read along with Education and Human Practices pages to see how environmental, economic, and social considerations are integrated throughout our project.

To implement these principles, the United Nations established the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in 2015 <Citation reference="united-nations-n-d" />. This framework provides 17 specific goals that address major global challenges, including poverty, hunger, clean energy, quality education, and climate action. The SDGs serve as a roadmap for coordinated international progress toward a more sustainable world by 2030.

However, the Sustainable Development Goals Report of 2026 shows that only over a third of the SDGs are on track to being achieved by the set deadline (WILL ADD CONNECTION, JUST found that report, quite fascinating).

For our iGEM Lund team, sustainability was a priority from the start. Representing Lund University, which ranks first globally in the Sustainability Rankings <Citation reference="qs-university-rankings-2026" />, we recognized our responsibility to align our project with these principles and contribute to helping bring SDGs closer to reality than they were before.

Recognizing that being specific and focused brings more benefit than wide but shallow approach, we targeted the specific SDGs found below in regards to our project.
