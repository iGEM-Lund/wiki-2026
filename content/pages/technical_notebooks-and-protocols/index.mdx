  **Main results of the week:**\\

  Plasmid Miniprep of *E. coli* TG1 cells containing pET-11a. Jenna Bioscience Plasmid Miniprep Kit instructions state how the cells could be resuspended in Lysis buffer by pipetting or vortexing, and we decided to test if there was a difference. By looking at the results, non-vortexed samples had higher DNA concentration, and resuspension by pipetting was done in future experiments. Samples were pooled, allowing for more convenient downstream applications. A260/280 were slightly higher than expected, but we still decided to use these samples for restriction digestion.



  | Sample                                 | **Concentration (ng/µL)** | **Abs 260/280** | Abs 260/230 |
  | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------- | ----------- |
  | Non-vortexed sample 1                  | 340                       | 2.079           | 2.243       |
  | Non-vortexed sample 2                  | 487                       | 2.081           | 1.972       |
  | Non-vortexed sample 2 (second reading) | 471                       | 2.097           | 2.257       |
  | Vortexed sample 3                      | 309                       | 2.073           | 2.383       |
  | Vortexed sample 4                      | 251                       | 2.053           | 1.996       |
  | Pooled sample                          | 347                       | 2.068           | 2.164       |



  Agarose gel visualisation of restriction digestion was done, without the holder for the gel. The ladder taken from last year's iGEM Team (New England Biolabs 1kb Plus Ladder DNA Ladder) was overloaded, and bands of digested pET-11a are slightly faint. In the future experiments, newly bought Ampliqon High Range DNA ladder can be used, along with keeping the gel in the holder.

  <FigureWithCaption src="https://static.igem.wiki/teams/6160/wiki/notebooks-and-protocols/18-06-gel-named.avif" caption="Figure: Restriction digestion of pET-11a with NdeI and BamHI, analysed by agarose gel electrophoresis. Lanes: (1) undigested pET-11a, (2) NdeI control, (3) BamHI control, (4) NEB 1kb Plus DNA Ladder, (5-8) double digested pET-11a." />

  Gel extracted plasmid concentrations were too low to be measured using NanoDrop, thus microcuvettes were used. Since the sample volume was 30 μL, they were diluted to 70 μL using MQ water. Their concentration is too low to be used for transformations, so restriction digestion will be repeated next week, with prolonged incubation time.

  | **Sample**                | **Concentration (ng/µL)** | **Abs 260/280** | **Abs 260/230** |
  | ------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
  | 1                         | 0.30                      | 3.0             | 0.035           |
  | 2                         | 0.15                      | 3.0             | 0.023           |
  | 3                         | 0.65                      | 3.250           | 0.046           |
  | pET-11a (5 times diluted) | 67.45                     | 2.063           | 2.279           |

  Competent E. coli TG1 cells were prepared, with a total of 125 tubes. They will be tested next week, to make sure they are competent for transformations.
