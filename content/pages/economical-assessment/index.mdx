## Overview

An economic assessment is an evaluation of the feasibility of a product considering its target market and consumers, production costs, and price estimation. It is useful for analyzing the costs and benefits of a project to determine whether it is feasible outside the lab and can be sold in the market.&#x20;

A complete economic assessment for a product like AnTigone, a genetically modified living biotherapeutic (LBT) aimed at reducing androgen levels locally in the gut for a systemic effect, would typically include:

* Market analysis
* SWOT/PESTLE analyses
* Comparator evaluation
* Regulatory pathway considerations
* Cost analysis
* Scale-up economics
* Price estimation
* Business model
* Equity and accessibility considerations
* Funding and risks

However, given our resources and early stage as a student team, our economic assessment primarily focused on comparative analysis and price analysis.&#x20;

## Assessment

### Comparative Analysis

The comparative analysis is an evaluation of already-existing products in the market. In this section, the competitive landscape is examined by comparing AnTigone with current PMOS treatments used in Sweden to answer the question: how does AnTigone compare with PMOS treatments already used by patients, and where does it fit among the existing treatment options?

Current treatments for PMOS focus on combatting symptoms after they are already present and include off-label anti-androgen or insulin sensitizer drugs (Spironolactone, Metformin), combined hormonal contraceptives (Leverette, Dizmine, Yasmin, Drovelis), GLP-1 receptor agonists (Wegovy, Saxenda, Mounjaro), and dietary supplements (Myo-inositol, Probi, Nupure). Traditional treatments for PMOS act systemically in the body and include metformin, spironolactone, and combined contraceptive pills, which have a monthly price range of SEK 34-135, while weight-management medications range from SEK 2,600-5,200 without subsidy. On the other hand, our product acts locally in the gut to induce a systemic effect, lowering testosterone levels throughout the body. In order for our product to compete with the comparators mentioned above, a monthly price of SEK 300-1000 would be a reasonable target, as it is greater than that of generic drugs and over-the-counter supplements but lower than that of the weight-management drugs. However, the price for our product has not yet been determined, and a preliminary price range will be estimated following further assessment and taking into account production and regulatory considerations. 

### Price Analysis

#### Product Classification and Pricing Approach

The proposed approach for PMOS is an oral capsule containing live, genetically engineered bacteria. As the product is intended for a therapeutic purpose, it will be developed as a live biotherapeutic product (LBT/LBP). Because the bacterial strain is genetically modified, additional safety assessments and GMO-related regulatory requirements may apply in Sweden and the EU. The final regulatory classification and applicable requirements would need to be confirmed with the relevant authorities ([Environmental risk assessments for medicinal products containing, or consisting of, genetically modified organisms (GMOs) - Scientific guideline | European Medicines Agency (EMA)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/scientific-guidelines/environmental-risk-assessments-medicinal-products-containing-or-consisting-genetically-modified-organisms-gmos)), ([Frontiers | Live Biotherapeutic Products, A Road Map for Safety Assessment](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2020.00237/full)).

This price analysis compares three product categories: conventional probiotic supplements, non-GMO live biotherapeutic products (LBTs), and genetically modified LBTs (GMO-LBTs). Swedish products are used as the primary market benchmarks, with European and US examples included where relevant. As no directly comparable marketed product for the proposed PMOS treatment has been identified, an illustrative cost model is used to estimate its potential price. 

#### **Price progression and main cost drivers**

**Conventional probiotic supplements**

Conventional probiotics provide a reference point for consumer pricing in Sweden. For example, Apoteket lists Probi Original (80 capsules) at a retail price of SEK 229. This price represents what consumers pay for the finished product and does not indicate its manufacturing cost. ([Köp Probi Mjölksyrabakterier Original, 80 st | Apoteket.se](https://www.apoteket.se/produkt/probi-original-80-st-ask-1564158/) ).

The main costs of conventional probiotics include bacterial cultivation, capsule formulation, packaging, quality control and distribution ([The Production and Delivery of Probiotics: A Review of a Practical Approach - PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6463069/) ).
