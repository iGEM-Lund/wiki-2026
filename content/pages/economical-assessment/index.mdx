## Overview

An economic assessment is an evaluation of the feasibility of a product considering its target market and consumers, production costs, and price estimation. It is useful for analyzing the costs and benefits of a project to determine whether it is feasible outside the lab and can be sold in the market.&#x20;

A complete economic assessment for a product like AnTigone, a genetically modified living biotherapeutic (LBT) aimed at reducing androgen levels locally in the gut for a systemic effect, would typically include:

* Market analysis
* SWOT/PESTLE analyses
* Comparator evaluation
* Regulatory pathway considerations
* Cost analysis
* Scale-up economics
* Price estimation
* Business model
* Equity and accessibility considerations
* Funding and risks

However, given our resources and early stage as a student team, our economic assessment focused on three areas: competitive landscape, cost analysis, and price estimation.&#x20;

## Assessment

### Comparative Analysis

The comparative analysis is an evaluation of already-existing products in the market. In this section, the competitive landscape is examined by comparing AnTigone with current PMOS treatments used in Sweden to answer the question: how does AnTigone compare with PMOS treatments already used by patients, and where does it fit among the existing treatment options?

Current treatments for PMOS focus on combatting symptoms after they are already present and include off-label anti-androgen or insulin sensitizer drugs (Spironolactone, Metformin), combined hormonal contraceptives (Leverette, Dizmine, Yasmin, Drovelis), GLP-1 receptor agonists (Wegovy, Saxenda, Mounjaro), and dietary supplements (Myo-inositol, Probi, Nupure). Table 1 summarizes the comparators, their role in PMOS treatment, and monthly treatment costs, along with the dosage used as a basis for cost estimation. Traditional treatments for PMOS act systemically in the body and include metformin, spironolactone, and combined contraceptive pills, which have a monthly price range of SEK 34-135, while the weight management medications range from SEK 2,600-5,200 without subsidy. On the other hand, our product acts locally in the gut to induce a systemic effect, lowering testosterone levels throughout the body. The price for our product has not yet been determined, but an estimated price range will be established upon further assessment.
