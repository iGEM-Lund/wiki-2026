**DTU BioBrick Workshop 2026**&#x20;

2026.04.17 - 2026.04.19

Huge thanks to DTU BioBuilders for hosting the BioBrick Workshop 2026 and bringing the iGEM community together!

Together with iGEM teams from Aarhus, Oslo, Stockholm, Copenhagen, SDU, BKEA, and DTU, we shared our project ideas and experiences through team pitches and discussions. The workshop then took us from ideas to practice, with wet lab sessions covering cloning, transformation, plating, and other essential techniques, while dry lab sessions introduced tools and methods for project design and planning. Along the way, we also had the opportunity to meet experts from different fields and iGEM Ambassadors, whose advice helped answer our questions and gave us a clearer direction for the season ahead.

Beyond learning, a special highlight was bringing The Sequence to DTU! We recorded an in person episode with iGEM Oslo and SDU, as well as a DTU Special with DTU BioBuilders, turning an inspiring workshop into an opportunity for even more collaboration and connection!!

European MeetUp-Málaga

2026.07.14 -2026.07.16

Warmly welcomed by iGEM UMA, we spent several exciting days together with iGEM Leiden, KU Leuven, and Bielefeld, sharing ideas, learning from each other, and experiencing the energy of southern Spain.

Throughout the meetup, we met experts from different fields and had the chance to speak directly with an iGEM judge, whose answers to our competition related questions helped us better prepare for the months ahead. UMA also took us beyond the competition, from enjoying the Spanish sun together at the beach to visiting the local Victoria brewery, giving us plenty of time to explore Málaga and build closer connections with the other teams.

At the Mini Jamboree, we practiced presenting our project and learned from other teams and the judges’ feedback. Even more excitingly, iGEM Lund received the **Best Overall Project Award!** 🏆 This gives us even more confidence and motivation for the months ahead.

And of course, we could not miss the chance to bring The Sequence along! 🎙️ We recorded an in person episode with the amazing iGEM Leiden, turning another meetup into a new podcast collaboration.

We also collaborated with iGEM ETH Zurich on their YouTube series, "[Proof It's Not Always Hard](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUqxkL0zQMoQ\&si=vgOH-gsBTfkXX3pg)," featuring a vlog of our Málaga adventure! 🎥 Through this collaboration, we wanted to show another side of iGEM beyond the lab, capturing the friendships, cultural experiences, and unforgettable moments that make this journey so special.

## Podcast by iGEM Lund 2026 **- [The Sequence](https://open.spotify.com/show/1anr7zYZ3pKdntKfGcVEvz?si=j3_TYq5HQr-aZCCynVjoTQ\&utm_source=copy-link)**[🎙️](https://open.spotify.com/show/1anr7zYZ3pKdntKfGcVEvz?si=j3_TYq5HQr-aZCCynVjoTQ\&utm_source=copy-link)

**Let’s Talk Science!**

The Sequence is a podcast created by iGEM Lund 2026 to connect with iGEM teams from around the world. Through our WorldWide Wednesday series, we invite different teams to share their projects, experiences, challenges, and journeys in iGEM.

Beyond scientific discussions, we aim to bring the global iGEM community closer together, exchange ideas, and highlight the people and stories behind the science!
